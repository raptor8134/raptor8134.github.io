Fatigue-resistant lattice inserts

james@notley.eng
notley.eng

Redesigned a titanium lattice insert for a spinal cage so it survives 10⁶ cycles at body temperature without stress shielding.

Build orientation drove nearly all of the scatter in fatigue life. Struts printed at 45° carried the largest unmelted-powder defects, so the lattice was re-oriented to put every load-bearing strut within 20° of vertical.

Hot isostatic pressing closed the remaining gas porosity and moved failure initiation from internal defects to the surface, where it can be controlled by chemical polishing.

Room temperature, 10 Hz, R = 0.1, n = 6 per condition. Fatigue per ASTM E466.
